Start here, before anything else: get on the City’s waitlist. You must be on the City of Ottawa’s Child Care Registry and Waitlist to get a space in licensed child care. Waits are often around 2 years, so families register early, even during pregnancy. Log in at least every 60 days or your application can become inactive.
